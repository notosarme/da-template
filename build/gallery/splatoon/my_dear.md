happy 10th

i sat on this idea for a year. Splatoon is a top 3 game series for me, i've found so much love and joy in it and its community over this decade. like a teenager it's had its growing pains but it's really bloomed into something spectacular, set a high bar, and imo is Nintendo's best series. funny to think about all that 2015 stateside marketing now, very odd but very memorable. thank you to all of my friends who have played with me, and my family who reveled in my obsession!

about the plushies: i've had them since early 2016 and used to photograph them on summer trips. they've since retired but that history is precious and lined up with the purpose of this painting

about the cloth: one of my blankets. it wraps around them warmly

about the shell: from one of my beach trips back then

12" x 12", acrylic on canvas

time: a week (stretching/gessoing canvas and painting)
