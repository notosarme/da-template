it pays off to be the King's friend, sometimes

where do i start with this, my biggest painting of the year so far... i started it sometime before 4/27 but wasn't in a hurry to have it done by then; i built my PC on 4/27 anyhow. this became the first artwork completed on it and my new, larger, better monitor.

i had two untrelated gkb ideas floating around: one of a fireworks show outside of kirby's home, and another of warp star and dedede. combining them made the vision much more grand but also technical. i used a MMD model of a mansion to capture the correct angle of the castle, and scrutinized its scale thru all of its depictions in the games. it's used as a visual landmark of sorts, not to any realistic scale, yet my end result is a bit small.

the main 6 made sense to be included, but i wanted to draw someone i haven't before, so i threw in lolo and lala. in the gkb world, they are demon beasts ordered by dedede in the past. they're harmless and utilized as little butlers. the job has perks...

warp star and dedede are drinking tea and booze.
